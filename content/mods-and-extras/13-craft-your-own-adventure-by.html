Craft Your Own Adventure! by Telltale Games

Craft Your Own Adventure was a small YouTube video released on the Telltale Games YouTube channel, where you got to play a little demo of a choice from Season Two Episode 1. It played similarly to The Netflix Archive, using the YouTube end screen feature to allow "choices." Depending on who you picked it would give different dialogue from each character.
