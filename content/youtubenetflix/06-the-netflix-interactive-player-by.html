The Netflix Interactive Player By Eveep23

The Netflix Interactive Player was designed by Eveep23 for the Netflix Interactives that were delisted at the end of 2024, but eventually had support for MCSM added. It supports all episodes, all genders and all languages (although still only English/Latin Spanish subtitles). Unlike the Netflix Archive, these are playable offline, but take up a fair bit of hard drive space. Additionally, this is designed for solely for Windows.

1. Obtain the "Internal Videos". These are the full episodes with all paths as a video, downloadable from the MCSM Netflix Archive Discord, or the Interactive Archive Discord. Additionally, you can add the language files (obtainable from the MCSM Netflix Archive Discord) to the video with MKVToolNix.

2. Download the Interactive Player from Github. Go to Releases and download the installer or .zip (for portable use).

3. Extract and run the Interactive Player.exe. In the menu, navigate to Minecraft Story Mode and select it. It should open a menu showing episodes 1-5. Simply click "Install", then navigate to and select the episode video file. You can also configure the language (if you added the language audio track) in the player's settings.
