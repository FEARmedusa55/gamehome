Is this safe? Will I get a virus?

One of the reasons the megathread was created was because there is a lot of misinformation and viruses about on the internet, especially for a game like Minecraft: Story Mode where it can't be downloaded through official means anymore. We wanted to create an easy to understand, risk free resource. Everything on the megathread has been thoroughly tested and is used by many. If it wasn't safe, it wouldn't be on the website in the first place. All that is required is that you at least have a bit of understanding as to what you're doing, which is why actually reading the website is so important.
