Legitimately

Physical

You can buy the physical PC disc off of third-party retailers like Amazon or eBay. Season One's (Season Pass) and Season Two’s PC physical copy does not contain episodes 2-5 and there is no way to download the episodes without pirating them.

Steam Keys

Steam keys are a mixed bag. Steam key grey markets have a shady reputation, so we can't recommend it highly. Prices are often outrageous, going for up to $250 to $300. It usually isn't worth the money or risk, and you’ll be supporting scalpers who sell the game for exorbitant prices. 

However, if you’re insistent, buy a key from 

  • G2A

  • Loaded (Formerly CDKeys) (usually sold out)
