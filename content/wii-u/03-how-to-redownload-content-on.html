How to redownload content on Wii U eshop Click to expand

  1. From the Wii U Menu, select the Nintendo eShop icon and tap Open.

  2. Select My Menu or press the Y Button.

  3. Select Your Downloads and the type of content being redownloaded (Software or Content).

  4. Select Downloaded Software or Downloaded Content.

  5. Scroll through the list of titles, and select Download next to the title you wish to redownload.

    • If the Download option is not visible, this indicates the software is already installed on your Wii U console. Installed software can be found on the Wii U Menu.

    • If the selected content has a rating, it will be displayed on the screen. Select Next to continue.

  6. Select Download.

  7. Select Next, and then select Download again.

  8. The download will begin automatically. Select Continue to return to the Nintendo eShop Menu.
