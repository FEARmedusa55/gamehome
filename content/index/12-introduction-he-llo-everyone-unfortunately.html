Introduction

Hello everyone,


Unfortunately, nothing built can last forever. So eventually, Minecraft: Story Mode was removed from all stores on July 25th, 2019.


This guide covers both "legitimate" and "unofficial" means of obtaining the game. The decision is entirely up to you, the end user, but it's generally recommended to take the "unofficial" path, as many platforms aren't possible officially in any way anymore. This site will also double as a console modding thread! (sometimes)


Let’s get started!




The different versions of the game

A normal disc that just says “Minecraft Story Mode” contains the first 5 episodes of Season 1. These episodes are not on the disc apart from Episode 1. So if you’re purchasing for a console with no support for redownloading Telltale's episodes, (currently only Xbox 360), you won’t be able to play the other episodes.


There is/was a digital version of episodes 6-8 called the Adventure Pass. It can/could be applied to the mobile version of the game, (no longer possible, not even when restoring a purchase), and any “Season Pass” version of the game, (no longer possible). Also worth mentioning that it is viewable on the PS4 store and Xbox One store as well, although it is not purchasable.


“Complete Adventure” disc/cartridge contains all the episodes for Season 1. (Only version available of Season 1 for Wii U and Switch) this version contains all of the Episodes on disc, so buying for Xbox 360 will grant you all the Season 1 episodes on-disc.


“Minecraft Story Mode Season Two” disc/cartridge is the entire second season of the game. The episodes need to be downloaded (with the exception of Nintendo Switch and the Japanese release on PS4), so again, if you’re on a console with no support for Telltale's episodes (Xbox 360) you can only play episode one unless you modify your console. This is not available for any PS3 or Wii U system.


For digital copies, the DLC episodes (6 through 8) are referred to as the "Adventure Pass."
