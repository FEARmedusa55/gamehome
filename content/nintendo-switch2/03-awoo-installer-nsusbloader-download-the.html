Awoo Installer + NS-USBLoader


  1. Download the game, and the software. duh.

And use a good software like 7Zip to extract both games :)


  1. Once downloaded, go onto your switch and hold R while launching into a software. Awoo Installer doesn't properly work while in Applet Mode. Then on your computer, open NS-USBLoader and plug your console into your computer via a USB. (You can install over LAN if you don't have a USB. It's just slower.)


  1. Drag and Drop the .XCI or .NSP file you're trying to install into NS-USBLoader, and now on Awoo Installer click Install over USB. Once you've connected your console to your computer, click Upload to NS on NS-USBLoader.


NOTE: Make sure you've Downloaded and Installed the correct Drivers for NS-USBLoader! You can do so my going into the settings, also make sure you've selected Awoo in the top left. (You can also remove all the "anime-y" stuff in the settings of Awoo Installer. I wouldn't blame you.)


  1. Once they appear on your console's screen, install them to the SD Card. You CAN install them to the system, but it's not really recommended.


  1. Once installed, read the funny message and safely exit out the app by clicking OK -> Exit. (simple)


DBI + DBI Backend

Note: There hasn't been an English release in a while. You can either look at the GitHub for a translation of all the UI, or download the Last English Version (Version 658)

  1. Download the game, and the software. duh.

And use a good software like 7Zip to extract both games :)

  1. Once downloaded, on your switch make sure you hold R while launching into a game and go into DBI. Most apps (Like this) don't properly work while in Applet Mode. Then on your computer, open DBI Backend and plug your console into your computer via a USB. (You can install over a server if you don't have a USB. It's just slower.)

  2. In DBI Backend, click on Add files and add the season you are trying to install. Then, click on start server. Now on your switch, click on "Install title from DBIbackend".


Pro Tip (Optional, but Helpful): Every time you boot into the game it asks you to link a Nintendo Account you can bypass this message for this and ALL software by generating a FAKE Nintendo Account using Linkalho!
