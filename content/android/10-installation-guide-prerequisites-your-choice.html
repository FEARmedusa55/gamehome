Installation Guide

Prerequisites

  • Your choice of a split package installer below

  • Enough storage space on your device

    • Do NOT, under ANY circumstance, close off the app or cancel the install while it's installing, or you may lose storage space!!!!!!!

      • Let the install complete or fail naturally, do not force things yourself! If you do, read the following.

        • Uninstalling the Split APK Installer can be hit or miss, on some devices it worked, on others not so much, I would advise against it.

        • Wait for 3 days (I recommend waiting 4), and finally reboot your phone. [Source]
