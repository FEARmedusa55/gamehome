The Netflix Archive    Episodes 1-5     

The Netflix Archive is a YouTube channel that's main goal is to archive the version of the game that was on Netflix until it got taken down on December 4th, 2022. Fortunately, AsteroidBK and SrBig archived the videos and have since been adapted to YouTube as a very basic point and click adventure using the end screen function on YouTube. The trailers and YouTube playable episodes were made and archived by Pox1016. Along with the game itself, the YouTube channel has also archived many things such as media related to the Netflix version and other languages. So far, episodes 1 - 5 have been ported, but only as male Jesse as of now. Female Jesse is in development right now, and you can view progress on the Netflix Archive Discord server.

How to save your path!

The first time you want to save the game, click "Save" under the video, and create a new playlist. Name it what you like, we are going to be treating these playlists like save files (so if you wanted to have another save file, you'd create another playlist, etc). Then save the video to said playlist. Now, when you want to save, just click "Save" under whichever video you're on, and add it to the playlist. Then, whenever you come back, click the latest video under that playlist, and boom, you have your path. No need to do this in between episodes either, as at the end of each episodes it takes you to the next episode WITH your path.
